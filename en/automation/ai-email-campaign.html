AI Email Campaigns

Personal emails written by AI for every contact

Built and testedHiringContent & messagingPersonalised outreach2026

Fill in a form with a contact list and, for a job search, your CV. Each contact gets an email written for them, sent one at a time through Gmail with a random pause, and you get a report when it is done.

What it handles

  • Arabic or English column headers are mapped automatically
  • Your CV's text is given to the model as context for job-search emails
  • Every send is recorded as sent or failed
