WhatsApp Booking Bot

Shop bookings through buttons, built with n8n

Built and testedFor shopsBooking bot for shops2026

A customer taps a service, then a time slot, in WhatsApp buttons. The confirmed slot is written to Google Calendar and confirmed back on WhatsApp.

What it handles

  • Service and time chosen with buttons, not typing
  • Every booking lands in Google Calendar
  • The customer gets a confirmation on WhatsApp
Trigger
WhatsApp message
Services
  • n8n
  • WhatsApp
  • Google Calendar
