Unified Inbox

Customer messages, with AI-drafted replies

Built and testedContent & messagingInbox with AI triage2026

Messages from every channel land in one inbox. Each one is enriched with its dialect, intent, priority and a summary, plus a draft reply written in the sender's own dialect. Replies leave through an outbox that records every result.

What it handles

  • Greetings and emoji-only messages never reach the model: they are classified locally to save cost
  • Every channel is normalised to one contract and written with a single atomic call
  • Enrichment continues on error, so no message is ever lost
  • The outbox sends one message at a time and records a readable failure reason
Trigger
Incoming message, webhook and schedule
Services
  • n8n
  • OpenAI
  • Supabase
  • Telegram
  • Structured outputs
