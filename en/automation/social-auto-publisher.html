Social Auto-Publisher

Posts with AI captions and branded images

Built and testedContent & messagingFor shopsAI social media studio2026

Send a post idea to a Telegram bot and get back captions for each platform and a branded image in your own colours, fonts and logo. Nothing is scheduled until you approve the preview.

What it handles

  • The brand kit is stored once and edited through chat commands
  • A vision pass picks the text zone, logo corner and overlay strength for each background
  • The final image is composed as HTML and rendered to PNG
Trigger
Telegram message
