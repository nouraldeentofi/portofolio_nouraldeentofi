Lesson Quality Evaluator

Lesson videos scored for teaching quality

Built and testedEducationVideo-to-data webhook service2026

A webhook service that transcribes lesson videos with speaker separation and returns structured JSON scoring teacher quality and student engagement.

What it handles

  • Speaker diarization separates teacher from student
  • Structured outputs guarantee a parseable result every time
  • Returns a score rather than a transcript, so the caller gets data, not homework
Trigger
Webhook (video upload)
Services
  • n8n
  • AssemblyAI Speaker Diarization
  • OpenAI GPT-4.1 Structured Outputs
