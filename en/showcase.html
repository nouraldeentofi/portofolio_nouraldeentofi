Websites I design and build

I am an independent web developer in Al Khobar. I offer website design and development, in Arabic, English or both, for professionals, companies, shops and restaurants in Dammam, Dhahran and the rest of Saudi Arabia. Each site starts from who they are, not from a template.

What you get

  • A site designed around you or your business, not a template with your name on it.
  • Arabic, English or both, each reading in its own direction.
  • Fast on phones, where most of your visitors are.
  • Readable by Google and by AI assistants, so people who ask about you find you.
  • For shops and restaurants: what you sell or serve shown in photos, the location on the map, and ordering or booking through WhatsApp.

Where I work

I meet clients in person in Al Khobar, Dammam and Dhahran. For clients elsewhere in Saudi Arabia and abroad, the whole project runs remotely.
