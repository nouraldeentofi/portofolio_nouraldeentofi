Nour Motion Studio

Free motion-graphics video maker in the browser

Motion-graphics studio2026

Pick a size and a style, add ready-made scenes, and export a motion video for free, in the browser, with no account and no server.

Visit live site (opens in a new tab)

The problem

Making a short motion video for LinkedIn, Reels, or YouTube usually means a paid subscription, a professional editor that takes weeks to learn, or an online service that keeps your project and your files.

What I built

An Arabic, right-to-left studio that runs entirely in the browser, with no API key, no server, and no subscription. A short wizard asks for the size and the style, then for a ready video idea or hand-picked scenes. Every template previews large and playing before it is added, and can be added as a new scene, merged into the current one, or swapped in for it. All of a scene's text sits in one place, a double tap writes straight onto the video, and a quick toolbar over any element enlarges, shrinks, recolours, animates, copies, or deletes it. Around that: a searchable library of animated backgrounds, including the living node mesh from this site; transitions previewed between your own scenes; camera moves with parallax depth; sound effects generated in the browser, plus your own voice-over; any moment saved as a PNG or JPG poster; and video themes, with day and night modes for the studio itself. The video is encoded on the visitor's own device with WebCodecs, falling back to real-time WebM recording where encoding is not supported. Projects autosave in the browser, and every frame carries a soft diagonal watermark that only the owner's password removes.
