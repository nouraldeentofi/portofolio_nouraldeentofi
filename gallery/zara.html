Who Am I?

I turn complex systems into games people actually want to play.

Born in Lagos, raised on early PlayStation demos, now based in Amsterdam. I got into game dev by reverse-engineering Flash games at age 12 and never looked back. My approach is to treat every mechanic like a mini-language — it should feel intuitive, surprising, and inevitable all at once.

Beyond games I do creative coding, contribute to open-source tooling for indie devs, and occasionally make generative art that I sell as prints.
