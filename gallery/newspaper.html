Engineer Ships Again; Accepts New Commissions.

Walter Finch, a full-stack developer operating out of Europe, was reported to have accepted new client work this morning — citing "an unshakeable appetite for clean interfaces and honest code."

By Staff Correspondent Filed 04:42 GMT — Desk of Engineering

In a development likely to interest founders, design leads, and any team with a React codebase older than a fiscal year, full-stack engineer Walter Finch has officially reopened his books for 2026.

Mr. Finch, whose career began in 2019 and who has since been quietly responsible for a trail of shipped products, design systems, and AI-assisted interfaces, told this paper he remains "obsessed with the last ten percent" — the polish most projects never quite reach.

His arsenal, the paper has confirmed, includes React, TypeScript, Node.js, Python, and a growing shelf of Claude-powered tooling. Witnesses describe his interfaces as "fast, considered, and genuinely pleasant to use."

Asked whether rumours of a long waitlist were accurate, Mr. Finch said only: "If the brief is clear, and the work is honest — we can make time."
