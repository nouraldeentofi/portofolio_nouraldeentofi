Chapter the Second

An Introduction

In which the proprietor is presented to the reader, the dossier opened, and certain particulars committed to the record.

Plate I — Self-Portrait, MMXXVI

Mr. Gustave Beaumont

Of mixed coat — the engineer's and the artist's — the proprietor of this small establishment has spent some seven years between the design studio and the server room. He finds the journey reasonable, & reports no quarrels between the two parties.

The Beaumont Hotel is presently in service to clientele of all dispositions: enterprise, startup, & the otherwise particular. The proprietor receives commissions by appointment, & replies, as a rule, by the next post.
