Liner Notes

The story behind the record.

Pressed RJ 2026 · Vol I

This record was written in quiet hours — between cups of coffee, commits at midnight, and the soft hum of a machine thinking alongside me. Every track is a problem I fell in love with long enough to ship.

My work sits where engineering meets craft: full-stack systems that feel considered from the first paint to the last query. I care about typography the way a mastering engineer cares about the final 2 dB. I care about state the way a drummer cares about timing.

I build with React, TypeScript, Node, Python, and a growing shelf of AI tools. But the real instrument is curiosity — and the willingness to re-cut a track until it sings.

— Ruby, producer & engineer
