// about me

Design is a conversation
between form and intention.

I'm Sofia Marchetti, a brand designer and motion artist with 9+ years of experience helping companies find their visual voice. My work sits at the intersection of strategy and craft — every mark, every frame, every colour choice serves a purpose.

Previously at Pentagram and Wolff Olins. Now independent, working with startups, cultural institutions, and global brands who want design that actually means something.
