CHAPTER I

A Preface from the Naturalist

In which the author offers his terms of address — and the reader is asked, kindly, to attend.

Permit me, gentle reader, this small indulgence: to set down in one volume the specimens of seven years' labour, gathered from many gardens — some cultivated, some wild — and to render each, as faithfully as the engraver's plate allows, for your study.

I am a developer of full stack and a creative technologist, residing presently in the city of San Francisco. My profession bids me wear two coats at once: that of the engineer, fond of the cool logic of databases and the careful seam of a well-formed API; and that of the artist, who can spend a morning tuning the cadence of a single micro-interaction.

The works herein catalogued span platforms commercial and projects open-handed; mobile applications and instruments of generative art; tools for the enterprise and tools for the curious. Some you may use today. Others have passed into the quiet of completed engagements.

“I obsess over micro-interactions as much as database schemas — and I see no contradiction in it.”

In what follows you will find: plates of the principal specimens, with their habits and habitats; a taxonomy of the skills brought to bear upon them; a brief log of the expeditions of my career; and, at the close, an invitation to correspond — which I hope you will accept.

I remain, as ever, yours in code & ink,

R. Ellis
San Francisco — Vernal Equinox, MMXXVI
