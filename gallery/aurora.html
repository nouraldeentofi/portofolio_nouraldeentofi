// about

A hybrid practice — design, code, and quiet craft.

I grew up obsessed with two things: how things look and how things feel to use. Nine years later, that obsession turned into a hybrid practice — half design, half engineering.

I work closely with founders and product teams to make the messy middle of a software idea feel effortless. I'll sketch with you on a Tuesday and ship production SwiftUI by Friday.

When I'm away from the monitor, I bake sourdough, ride fixed-gear on bad roads, and collect design books I'll never fully read.

// what i do

Four things I do really well.

Pick one or bundle — most engagements blend two or three of these.

✦

Product Design

End-to-end product thinking — from first-principles research and information architecture to polished, production-ready interfaces.

◐

Interaction & Motion

I build the micro-interactions that make products feel alive. Curves, easings, gestural feedback — the details that separate okay from delightful.

◎

Design Systems

Tokens, components, docs, and governance. A system your team will actually use, not the one that dies in v0.3.1.

◈

Frontend Engineering

I hand off designs as code. React, SwiftUI, or Framer — whatever the product calls for. Always typed, always accessible.
