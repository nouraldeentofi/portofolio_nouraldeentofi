About the pilot

A designer who reads too much sci-fi.

I've spent eight years building brand worlds, digital product experiences, and launch campaigns for studios working in entertainment, gaming, music, and luxury. I move fluently between strategy, visual identity, motion, and production-ready code.

My favorite briefs are the ones where the outcome feels slightly impossible at week one and obvious by week six. If you've got a story that deserves scale — and a team willing to aim high — we'll probably get along.

Outside the studio: astrophotography, slow cinema, and an unreasonable archive of tour posters. Currently based in Berlin; happy to travel.
