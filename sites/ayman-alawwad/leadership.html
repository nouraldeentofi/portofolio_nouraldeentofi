Full bio

Ayman Alawwad is a PMO and portfolio management leader with more than ten years of experience across portfolio governance, strategic initiatives, technology, infrastructure, construction and government environments in Saudi Arabia. He combines hands-on delivery with portfolio-level governance to strengthen accountability, improve executive visibility and support effective decision-making.

At TAHAKOM he led the end-to-end delivery of national-level smart traffic violation detection systems across multiple Saudi cities, working with government and private-sector stakeholders, multi-vendor environments and cross-functional teams of more than 17. Earlier, he delivered a SAR 28 million infrastructure project for the Ministry of Education two weeks ahead of schedule, and launched a Center of Excellence that supported his firm's upgrade from 3rd- to 1st-grade classification.

He has earned the PMP®, PMI-RMP® and P3O® certifications, and was selected among 60 of 1,200 candidates for an eight-month High Potential leadership program with modules from MIT, the National University of Singapore, ESCP Business School and Northwestern University's Kellogg School of Management. He holds a bachelor's degree in civil engineering from Imam Mohammad Ibn Saud Islamic University.
