How I work

Principles I write about and work by.

  1. 01

    Technology serves the organisation's goals

    Every technology plan starts from the goal it serves; the tools come after. That is how technology turns from a cost into lasting value.

  2. 02

    Planning is a strategic process

    Not a timeline or a list of requirements, but a clear vision, an understanding of user needs, risk analysis and efficient use of resources.

  3. 03

    Every change is tested before it spreads

    A change goes first to a test environment and a limited set of devices to learn its impact, and only then to the whole system.

  4. 04

    Manage resources wisely, not just build them

    Strong systems alone are not enough; capacity management is what lets an organisation scale steadily and stay stable at peak times.
