    Common questions

    Where is the shop?

    In Al Khobar, Madinat Al Ummal, on King Abdulaziz Road. Tap “Open the map” and Google Maps takes you straight there.

    How do I book a measuring appointment?

    Choose how many thobes, the day and the time in the booking form, and WhatsApp opens with a ready message for you to send us.

    Can I choose my thobe model before I come?

    Yes. In “Prepare your thobe” you choose the collar, cuffs, chest pocket, buttons and fabric colour. Your idea reaches us with the booking, and the tailor confirms it with you at the measuring.

    Do you tailor sudairi vests?

    Yes, we tailor men's sudairi vests too. Ask us on WhatsApp about the models and fabrics.

    How much does tailoring cost?

    The price depends on the fabric and the model you choose. Ask us on WhatsApp and we will reply.
