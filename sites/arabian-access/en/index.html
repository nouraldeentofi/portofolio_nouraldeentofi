Frequently asked questions

What is the process for business setup in Saudi Arabia?

It involves selecting the appropriate legal structure, obtaining approvals, completing registration and securing the relevant licences. Experienced consultants make sure each step is handled accurately and efficiently.

Can foreign investors set up a business in Saudi Arabia?

Yes, through foreign company registration in Saudi Arabia, subject to regulatory approvals and compliance requirements. Proper guidance ensures international businesses meet all legal obligations.

How long does foreign company registration take?

The timeline varies with the business activity, documentation and regulatory approvals. A structured approach reduces delays and keeps execution smooth.

Why is professional support important for business setup?

It ensures setup is completed in compliance with local laws, reducing risk and avoiding costly corrections later.

Can I have a consultation remotely?

Yes. We offer online appointments and on-site services, or you can visit our office in Al Malaz, Riyadh.

What are your working hours?
